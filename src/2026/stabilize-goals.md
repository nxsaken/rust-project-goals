## Motivation

### The status quo

The Project Goals program has changed a lot from the end of 2025 and throughout 2026. We switched from a six-moth to annual periods, we defined a process to add goals throughout the year and we added the Funding scaffolding which provided a way for people to ask for — and crucially receive — money to work on a particular goal.

However, these changes came really quickly, which made some people a bit wary of relying on the program for other things (such as running Lang experiments) and a lot of people are not aware of them altogether.

Given that we want to keep evolving the program (hopefully for the better!), we need to make the current processes more accessible, communicate any changes better and be mindful adding more churn.

In addition, it is important that the Goals team has a direct experience with the entire goal lifecycle process itself, so this goal is an attempt at "eating our own dog food".

### What we propose to do about it

The goals should serve people in the Project (who are either proposing their own, supporting someone else's or requesting funding) as well as outside (people who want to drive changes in Rust or fund a particular effort).

To that effect, they should be easy to find, easy to create and update, convenient to provide progress updates to, with all their processes and the full lifecycle easy to discover and follow.

There are two parts of the goals lifecycle we've talked about that we want to have a proper discussion about and finalise:

* **Defining the exact workflow for closing goals:** we're leaning towards closing them after some period of inactivity (defined as no updates), but we need to nail down the details e.g. how and when do we reach out to the owner before that happens, what's the process of reopening them, etc.
* **Settle on the plan for goal cycles**: Right now, we have annual cycles (we're in the 2026 goal cycle now), but we've seen lots of goals being open throughout the year so we're leaning towards dropping  the cycles entirely and have goals be a rolling program.

For both of these, there are, many details to hash out and feedback we need to gather from the Project members, people who used goals etc.
